Overview

A product designer who grew up in Pune, India, and followed a pull toward UX research and human-centered design all the way to a Master's at RIT. Cares less about pixel-perfect decks and more about whether real people can actually use what gets shipped.

Currently splits time between UX research, product design, and figuring out where AI genuinely helps rather than just impresses — prototyping in Framer, researching with real users, building in an afternoon what used to take weeks.
