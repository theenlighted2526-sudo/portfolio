Redesigning MyCourses to reduce assignment-related anxiety and improve daily clarity for students with inbuilt AI assistant

UX Research Interface Design Information Architecture

MyCourses buried deadlines and submission status behind clutter. I redesigned the dashboard around a clearer hierarchy and built in an AI assistant that surfaces what's due, submitted, and needs action — giving students daily clarity instead of last-minute scrambling.

002

Designing Tiger Track to bring clarity to on-campus finance tracking

UX Research UI Design Interaction Design FinTech

RIT students struggled to track expenses and understand where their money went — existing tools buried transaction details that mattered. I led research and design for Tiger Track, a transparent finance-tracking experience with real-time balances and low-balance alerts that helps students stay ahead of overspending.

003

StarPath — an AR-powered companion for exploring the night sky

UX Design AR/UI Design Product Strategy Information Architecture

Stargazing apps tend to overwhelm beginners or under-serve experienced astronomers. StarPath is a mobile app concept bringing real-time celestial event tracking, an AR-based sky map, and a community astrophotography gallery into one experience — helping anyone plan sessions and identify what they're looking at just by pointing their phone at the sky.
