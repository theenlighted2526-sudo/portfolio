Experimental Study Fall 2024

Impact of note-taking medium on memory retention

HCI Research Methods
Abstract

A controlled experimental study with 8 participants comparing paper-based (PB) and paperless (PL) note-taking during a slideshow lecture and follow-up quiz, measuring memory retention scores and completion time using Welch's t-test and the Mann-Whitney U test.

Key finding

Neither medium showed a statistically significant advantage — PB and PL scored similarly without notes (8.5 vs. 8.75) and with notes (9.75 vs. 9.0, p = 0.18), and completion times didn't differ significantly either (p = 1.0), suggesting both are similarly effective for short-term retention and task completion.
